Thank you to the authors who shared the following code, research, and design
ideas. Each credit names the contribution used in the engine.

| People | Published work | Contribution used in Byte Engine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ryan Fleury | [Multi-Core By Default](https://www.dgtlgrove.com/p/multi-core-by-default) | Alley's execution model: lanes run the same function, narrow to serial work, and share results before continuing in parallel. |
| Eric Lengyel | [Slug reference shaders](https://github.com/EricLengyel/Slug) | Curve and band data layout and the adapted coverage shader for UI text and filled paths. |
| Troy Sobotka and Benjamin Wrensch | [Minimal AgX Implementation](https://iolite-engine.com/blog_posts/minimal_agx_implementation) | Sobotka's AgX display transform and Wrensch's polynomial approximation used by the AgX tone mapper. |
| John Hable, James McLaren, and Stephen Hill | [Visibility Buffer Rendering with Material Graphs](https://filmicworlds.com/blog/visibility-buffer-rendering-with-material-graphs/) | Barycentric coordinates and derivatives from the published calculation, including McLaren and Hill's corrected gradients. |
| Evan Wallace | [Fast Rounded Rectangle Shadows](https://madebyevan.com/shaders/fast-rounded-rectangle-shadows/) | UI shadow integration that combines an analytic row integral with four samples along the other axis. |
| Brian Karis and Dimitar Lazarov | [Physically Based Shading on Mobile](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/blog/physically-based-shading-on-mobile) | Karis's environment BRDF approximation, which builds on Lazarov's work. |
| Csaba Kelemen and László Szirmay-Kalos | [A Microfacet Based Coupled Specular-Matte BRDF Model with Importance Sampling](https://doi.org/10.2312/egs.20011003) | The coupling that gives diffuse light the share of light the specular lobe doesn't reflect, on the way in and on the way out. |
| The ACES Project contributors | [ACES 2.0 output transforms](https://github.com/aces-aswf/aces-core) | The ACES 2.0 output transform for SDR Rec.709: its tone scale, color appearance model, chroma compression, and gamut compression, ported to the ACES 2.0 color-grading pass and to the script that computes its per-hue tables. |
| Stephen Hill, Matt Pettineo, and David Neubelt | [Baking Lab's ACES.hlsl](https://github.com/TheRealMJP/BakingLab/blob/master/BakingLab/ACES.hlsl) | Hill's fit of the ACES reference rendering and sRGB output transforms, used by the ACES tone mapper and the ACES color-grading pass. |
| John Hable | [Filmic Tonemapping Operators](http://filmicworlds.com/blog/filmic-tonemapping-operators/) and [Uncharted 2: HDR Lighting](https://gdcvault.com/play/1012459/Uncharted-2-HDR) | The Uncharted 2 filmic tone curve, its constants, and its exposure bias, implemented independently from the published formula for the Hable tone mapper. |
| Melissa O'Neill, Mark Jarzynski, and Marc Olano | [Permuted Congruential Generator (PCG)](https://www.pcg-random.org/paper.html) and [Hash Functions for GPU Rendering](https://jcgt.org/published/0009/03/02/) | O'Neill's 32-bit permutation, used as a particle hash as studied by Jarzynski and Olano. |
| Tom Duff, James Burgess, Per Christensen, Christophe Hery, Andrew Kensler, Max Liani, and Ryusuke Villemin | [Building an Orthonormal Basis, Revisited](https://jcgt.org/published/0006/01/01/) | The stable tangent frame used by CPU and GPU environment baking, diffuse screen-space sampling, and surfel GI's ray directions. |
| Mikko Mononen | [Simple Stupid Funnel Algorithm](https://digestingduck.blogspot.com/2010/03/simple-stupid-funnel-algorithm.html) | The funnel restart rule used to straighten navigation paths through portals. |
| Jorge Jimenez, Jose I. Echevarria, Belen Masia, Fernando Navarro, and Diego Gutierrez | [SMAA](https://github.com/iryoku/smaa) | The adapted spatial antialiasing shaders and their area and search lookup textures. |
| Jorge Jimenez | [Next Generation Post Processing in Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare](https://www.iryoku.com/next-generation-post-processing-in-call-of-duty-advanced-warfare) | The 13-tap bloom downsampling filter and interleaved gradient noise. |
| Alan Wolfe | [Interleaved Gradient Noise: A Different Kind of Low Discrepancy Sequence](https://blog.demofox.org/2022/01/01/interleaved-gradient-noise-a-different-kind-of-low-discrepancy-sequence/) | The discussion of temporal noise offsets and mapping scalar noise to two dimensions used by screen-space sampling. |
| Jure Triglav | [Surfel-based global illumination on the web](https://juretriglav.si/surfel-based-global-illumination-on-the-web/) and [webgiya](https://github.com/jure/webgiya) | The design and constants of surfel GI: its cascaded grid, the surfel economy, the spawn and despawn votes, ray guiding, and radial depth, adapted from webgiya's shaders. |
| Henrik Halén, Andreas Brinck, Kyle Hayward, and Xiangshun Bei | [Global Illumination Based on Surfels](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2021/index.html) | Surfels spawned on the surfaces the camera sees, kept in a spatial grid, and lit by rays, which surfel GI builds on. |
| Tomasz Stachowiak and Embark Studios | [kajiya](https://github.com/EmbarkStudios/kajiya) | The cascaded surfel grid around the camera, which webgiya's grid and surfel GI's follow. The temporal and spatial filters of kajiya's ray-traced diffuse GI, adapted by surfel GI's reconstruction: a history that counts its samples up to a limit and gives most of them up when the light changes past its variance, and an edge-aware spiral filter that shrinks as the history grows confident. The irradiance cache of kajiya, which allocates entries where rays look them up, limited by the lookup's bounce, as surfel GI spawns surfels where rays hit surfaces no surfel covers. Surfel GI also measures its quality and cost against kajiya. |
| Martin Roberts | [The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Quasirandom Sequences](https://extremelearning.com.au/unreasonable-effectiveness-of-quasirandom-sequences/) | The R2 sequence each surfel's cosine-weighted rays walk, so a surfel that traces a few rays a frame still covers its hemisphere evenly, and the per-frame offsets that animate SSGI's blue noise. |
| Robert Ulichney | [The void-and-cluster method for dither array generation](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.152707) | The method that generates the engine's tiling blue-noise texture, which picks SSGI's ray directions and march offsets. |
| Timothy Lottes | [Optimized Reversible Tonemapper for Resolve](https://gpuopen.com/learn/optimized-reversible-tonemapper-for-resolve/) | The reversible tone map surfel GI's spatial pass averages light through, as kajiya's spatial filter does, so a lone bright texel doesn't spread. |
| Colin Barré-Brisebois, Henrik Halén, Graham Wihlidal, Andrew Lauritzen, Jasper Bekkers, Tomasz Stachowiak, and Johan Andersson | [Hybrid Rendering for Real-Time Ray Tracing](https://ea.com/seed/news/hybrid-rendering-for-real-time-ray-tracing) | The multiscale mean estimator, as webgiya runs it, which averages the light each surfel's rays bring back. |
| Eric Veach and Leonidas J. Guibas | [Optimally Combining Sampling Techniques for Monte Carlo Rendering](https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/combine/) | Multiple importance sampling: the weights that turn surfel rays drawn from the guide or the cosine-weighted distribution back into one average of their light, and the weights that count only the cosine-weighted rays toward each surfel's hit fraction. |
| Christoph Peters and Reinhard Klein | [Moment Shadow Mapping](https://momentsingraphics.de/I3D2015.html) | The Hamburger four-moment bound, as webgiya runs it, which tells from a surfel's radial depth how much of its view a pixel lies behind. |
| Peter Shirley and Kenneth Chiu | [A Low Distortion Map Between Disk and Square](https://doi.org/10.1080/10867651.1997.10487479) | The concentric map that, lifted onto the hemisphere, splits each surfel's guide and radial depth into cells of equal solid angle. |
| Tomas Akenine-Möller, Cyril Crassin, Jakub Boksansky, Laurent Belcour, Alexey Panteleev, and Oli Wright | [Improved Shader and Texture Level of Detail Using Ray Cones](https://jcgt.org/published/0010/01/01/) | The ray cones that pick the texture levels surfel rays read where they hit. |
| Sébastien Hillaire | [A Scalable and Production Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering Technique](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf) | The multiple-scattering approximation used by the atmosphere renderer. |
| Box2D authors | [Contact Recycling](https://box2d.org/posts/2026/10/contact-recycling/) | Reusing contact geometry while two bodies remain close to their previous relative placement. |
| Gino van den Bergen | [Ray Casting against General Convex Objects with Application to Continuous Collision Detection](https://www.dtecta.com/papers/jgt04raycast.pdf) | The support-mapped convex ray cast used for rounded shapes. |
| Inigo Quilez | [2D distance functions](https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions2d/) | The distance-function formulations referenced by UI sector coverage and the closest-point calculation for quadratic curves. |
