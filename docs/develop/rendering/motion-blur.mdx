Read this page before you change the `motion-blur` pass, `render_passes/motion_blur.rs`, its shaders in
`assets/rendering/motion_blur`, or how the velocity stage decides to run. What the pass renders is specified under
[Motion blur](/docs/reference/rendering#motion-blur), and the target it reads under the `Velocity` row of
[Stages and targets](/docs/reference/rendering#stages-and-targets).

## Why a reconstruction filter

A blur that gathers each pixel along its own motion only can't blur a moving object past its edge: the still
background next to it has no motion to gather along, so the silhouette stays sharp. It also smears a moving
background over a still object in front of it. The pass follows McGuire, Hennessy, Bukowski, and Osman,
*A Reconstruction Filter for Plausible Motion Blur* (I3D 2012), with that paper's weights and its suggested depth
extent. Each pixel samples along the longest blur that can reach it, and each tap counts only as much as it
plausibly covers the pixel, by depth and by how far each blur reaches.

The first version of the pass derived velocity from the two frames' view and projection matrices with the translation
removed, so it blurred camera turns and zooms only. Reading `Velocity` blurs camera moves, moving meshes, and skinned
meshes through the same path temporal antialiasing reprojects through.

## How the pass is built

`render_passes/motion_blur.rs` turns the shutter and the sink's frame interval into the shutter fraction and records
three stages, each inside its own GPU counter:

- **Tile max** (`tile-max.besl`, 16 × 16 compute). One workgroup covers a 32 × 32 tile, each lane 2 × 2 pixels. It
  scales each pixel's motion to its blur, reduces each row of 16 lanes with lane shuffles, and the first lane reduces
  the 16 rows from workgroup memory into `tile_max`.
- **Neighbor max** (`neighbor-max.besl`, 8 × 8 compute, one lane per tile). Each tile takes the longest tile max of
  the 3 × 3 tiles around it into `neighbor_max`.
- **Gather** (`gather.besl`, a tile pass that writes `main`'s replacement). A pixel whose neighborhood blur is under
  half a pixel passes through. Otherwise it takes the configured taps along the neighborhood blur and weighs each
  with the paper's soft depth compare, cones, and cylinders.

Blur vectors in the tile buffers are already scaled by the shutter and held to the radius, so `Velocity` itself
stays the raw motion of a frame, which temporal antialiasing needs.

## How velocity is turned on

The pass reads `Velocity` through `RenderPassBuilder::read_from`, which records the read. The renderer gathers, for
each sink and frame, the targets that the sink's enabled passes read, and hands them to the scene pipelines through
`Sink::with_read_targets`. The visibility pipeline's velocity stage runs only when `Sink::reads` reports its target.
Bypassing motion blur and temporal antialiasing therefore removes the velocity stage's cost too.

## Rules a change must keep

- **Tile width bounds the radius.** A blur no longer than a tile reaches at most the next tile, which is why a 3 × 3
  neighborhood holds every blur that can cover a pixel. Keep `max_blur_radius` held to `TILE_WIDTH`, and change both
  together.
- **Blur is half the shutter's motion.** The gather spreads taps from −N to +N, so the blur vector is half the
  motion the shutter covers; scaling by the whole motion doubles the smear.
- **Keep `Velocity` raw.** Scale by the shutter in the motion blur shaders, never in the velocity stage.
- **Keep the subgroup shuffles in `main`.** The BESL VM runs shuffles only where every lane reaches them together.
- **Read velocity only through the builder.** A pass that binds `Velocity` without `read_from` leaves the stage off.

## Tests

`render_passes/motion_blur.rs` runs the three shaders in the BESL VM on a two-tile image: a still image and a closed
shutter pass through, a pan mixes two halves across their edge, a moving object blurs over a still background, a
still object stays sharp over a moving background, and the tile max keeps the longest blur held to the radius. On
macOS each shader also lowers through the platform compiler. `pipelines/visibility/tests/velocity.rs` checks that a
frame after a skipped velocity stage counts meshes as still.
