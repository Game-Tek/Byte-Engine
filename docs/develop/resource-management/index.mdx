Use this section when you change how Byte Engine bakes assets or stores and
loads resources. For the stable domain boundary and terminology, start with the
[resource-management reference](/docs/reference/resource-management). To bake
and ship an application's resources, follow
[Prepare resources](/docs/use/resources).

Choose a system:

- [Asset processing](/docs/develop/resource-management/assets): asset handlers,
  bake tracing, and what processing produces.
- [Resource storage and loading](/docs/develop/resource-management/resources):
  storage contracts, the ReDB backend, payload encoding, and readers.
- [BEMX material graphs](/docs/develop/resource-management/bemx): how
  material sources become graphs and graphs become shaders.
- [MaterialX documents](/docs/develop/resource-management/materialx): read a
  document, convert a material to BEMX, and fix one that fails to load.
