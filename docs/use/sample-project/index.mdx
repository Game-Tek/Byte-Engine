In this tutorial, you build your first Byte: a shooting gallery like the ones
at a fair. Three rows of bullseye targets slide past, each row the other way and
farther back than the one in front. You aim with the mouse and fire balls at
them. A target you hit tips back and falls down, and your score goes up.

You don't need to know anything about Byte Engine to follow along. Each step
tells you what to type, where it goes, and what you should see when you run the
application. If you're new to Rust, keep [the Rust book](https://doc.rust-lang.org/book/)
open beside you.

## What you learn

On the way, you use the systems most games need:

- **The application loop.** Open a window and run your game one tick at a time.
- **Meshes and materials.** Build every shape from code, and paint it with
  materials you write in small text files.
- **Lights.** Light the scene for the night with bulbs, lamps, and moonlight.
- **Physics.** Make targets that slide, swing on hinges, and fall when a ball
  hits them.
- **Input.** Turn the mouse, the keyboard, and a gamepad into actions your game
  responds to.

## Before you start

Complete the [setup guide](/docs/use/setup). When you finish it, you have a Rust
project that depends on Byte Engine, with an `assets` folder that links to the
engine's assets. This tutorial calls that project `my-game`; use your own
project's name wherever you see it.

## Steps

Follow the steps in order. Each one adds to the program from the step before.

<Step>

### [Build the booth](/docs/use/sample-project/build-the-booth)

</Step>
<Step>

### [Light the night](/docs/use/sample-project/light-the-night)

Hang a string of bulbs, add lamps and moonlight, and fill the air with haze.

</Step>
<Step>

### [Make the targets](/docs/use/sample-project/make-the-targets)

Draw a round target, then use physics to make rows of targets that slide and
swing on hinges.

</Step>
<Step>

### [Aim and fire](/docs/use/sample-project/aim-and-fire)

Turn the camera with the mouse and fire balls where you look.

</Step>
<Step>

### [Keep score](/docs/use/sample-project/keep-score)

Count the targets you knock down, and bring each row back around.

</Step>
</Steps>
